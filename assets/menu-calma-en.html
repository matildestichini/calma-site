How it works

Travel. Zone 1: included for visits from 2,500 MZN; below this, 300 MZN. Zone 2: 500 MZN. Zone 3: 700 MZN, with a minimum of 60 min of treatments. We always confirm your zone and the total before booking.

Zone 1 Baixa, Alto Maé, Polana Cimento, Sommerschield, Coop, Malhangalene, Malanga, Munhuana, Mafalala, Maxaquene A and B.

Zone 2 Maxaquene C and D, Urbanização, Polana Caniço, Airport, Mavalane, FPLM, Ferroviário, Chamanculo, Xipamanine, Unidade 7, Hulene A.

Zone 3 Costa do Sol, Triunfo, Laulane, Mahotas, Hulene B, Albazine, Magoanine, Malhazine. Zimpeto, depending on the location.

Matola, KaTembe and KaNyaka are not yet part of our service area.

Minimum per visit: 1,000 MZN in treatments.

Payment at the end, by card (POS), M-Pesa or bank transfer. We do not accept cash.

30% deposit for bookings over 2 hours, the four-hand massage and two or more clients at the same time.

Cancellation free up to 12 hours before (24 hours for bookings with a deposit), or by 8 pm the day before for morning appointments.

Hours: Monday to Saturday, 6 am to 8 pm (first appointment at 6.30 am); Sunday, 7 am to 6 pm (first appointment at 7.30 am). Your therapist arrives 10 min early to set up; the time shown is the treatment time.
